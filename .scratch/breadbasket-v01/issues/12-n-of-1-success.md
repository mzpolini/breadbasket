# What convinces us this works at n=1

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Audience: founder
Blocked by: —

## Answer

Three criteria, all observable in a single week of use.

**1. He gets in and talks to it, and is impressed.** No friction reaching the thing, and the exchange itself is good enough that his reaction is positive rather than dutiful.

**2. While talking to it, he sees the tool calls expressed as a visual flow — and it gives him ideas for more tool calls.** Note this is a *generative* criterion, not a comprehension one. Passing isn't "he understood what happened"; it's "he came back with things he wants it to do." That is a higher bar and a more interesting one, and it makes [The chat surface design](20-chat-surface-design.md) load-bearing for success rather than a polish concern.

**3. Afterwards he can see his inventory in a way that makes him want to use it again next week to prepare for the farmers market — instead of the paper notebook he uses now.**

## What criterion 3 changes

**The incumbent is a paper notebook**, and the job is *market prep*. Neither was in the north star, which framed the competition as stale e-commerce storefronts and the value as buyer-facing freshness. For this pilot the value is **farmer-facing**: replacing a notebook for a weekly planning task. Buyers are not in the room.

That re-weights two things. [The public availability view](09-public-availability-view.md) matters less than assumed — nobody is discovering food through it during the pilot. And **the farmer's own view of his inventory**, which sat in the fog as an optional extra, is the thing this criterion actually measures — now [The farmer's own inventory view](25-farmer-inventory-view.md).

It also sets the real bar: not "is this good software" but **"is this better than a notebook for getting ready for Saturday."** A notebook is fast, offline, works in a truck, needs no login, and never misparses anything. That is a stronger incumbent than it sounds.

## Caveats worth carrying

- **These are developer-defined, not founder-confirmed.** Criteria 1 and 2 are observable by watching him. Criterion 3 is a claim about *his* future behaviour, and only he can validate it — confirm it with him rather than assuming.
- **No failure condition or time bound was stated.** What would make him stop, and how long the pilot runs before a call gets made, are still open. Worth asking in the same conversation.
- **"Logs in easily" sits against a settled premise** — v0.1 has no login, just one secret URL per farm. Read it as "reaches it without friction", which the secret URL satisfies. Flagged in case a login was actually intended.

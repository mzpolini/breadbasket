# Units and quantity

Type: grilling
Status: open
Audience: founder
Blocked by: —

## Question

How does the founder actually talk about how much of something he has?

Produce quantity does not reduce to one unit. A bunch of carrots, a head of lettuce, tomatoes by the pound, eggs by the dozen, greens by the bushel — and often no number at all ("a good amount", "plenty", "just about out").

- What unit families exist in practice — weight, count, bunch, volume, area-picked?
- Which products are habitually spoken in which unit?
- How often is the quantity genuinely vague, and does a buyer need a number or just a signal that there is some?
- Is "how much" even worth capturing in v0.1, or is "yes / nearly out / no" enough?

## Why it matters

The agent cannot normalise into a shape nobody has defined. This is the founder's lived knowledge, not something to be guessed at from a dataset.

# Questions that outlive the exchange

Type: grilling
Status: open
Audience: us
Blocked by: —

## Question

The design models something we do not have: **a question the agent asked, that he never answered, still sitting there days later.**

> *"a mess of greens, 8 bags" — I didn't know that name, so I wrote nothing and asked. You never answered — the 8 bags are still not in the book."* · **Answer it now**

And for the unresolved conflict: *"Corn still isn't showing. Boxes tonight, dozens Tuesday. Two days unshown now."* · **Sort it out**

We have no record of a question, no unresolved state, and no way for an exchange to end open and be picked up on Tuesday.

- What **is** a pending question — a record with a prompt, an originating utterance, and a resolution? Or just a derived view over positions that cannot be totalled?
- Which situations create one? Unresolved product, unit conflict, a suspected silent discard, an empty parse — or only the ones where **stock is genuinely missing from the book**?
- Does answering it **write the withheld movement**, or start a fresh exchange? The design implies the former: the 8 bags are waiting to be written.
- How does it surface without becoming a notification? The design puts it in his own view as work, not as a nudge — consistent with the one-check-in-one-reminder rule.
- Does it expire?

## Why it matters

It is what stops an unresolvable conflict from being quietly forgotten. Without it, "boxes vs dozens" means corn is simply invisible to buyers forever and nobody is ever reminded — the failure mode is silence, which is the one this product exists to eliminate.

It is also what makes the session trace worth having rather than a log.

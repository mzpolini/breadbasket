# Freshness windows

Type: grilling
Status: open
Audience: founder
Blocked by: —

## Answered already

The **rhythm** half of this ticket was settled by the founder: a ping every **Sunday 7pm** to confirm the week's availability, and a reminder **7am Monday** if it goes unanswered. One check-in, one reminder — consistent with the north star's anti-spam rule.

That leaves the freshness half, which is a different question and stays open. Cadence is config, not schema, so these two never had to agree.

## Question

- **Freshness windows.** How long does a claim stay true, per product family? Salad greens go in days; winter squash sits for months. What is the founder's own sense of how fast each thing stops being true?
- **Who sets the window.** A sensible default per crop, or does the founder set it per item as he says it? The second is more honest and more friction.
- **Seasonal variation.** Does the window change by season for the same crop?
- **Does the rhythm hold year-round?** Sunday 7pm is a reasonable default, but peak season and deep winter are very different weeks. Is the schedule fixed or does he want it to move?

## Why it matters

Get these wrong in one direction and real food is hidden; wrong in the other and the product breaks its only promise. They also produce [When freshness runs shorter than the rhythm](15-freshness-shorter-than-rhythm.md), which is where the two halves collide.

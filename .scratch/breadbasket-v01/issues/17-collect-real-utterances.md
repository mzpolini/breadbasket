# Collect how the founder actually says it

Type: task
Status: open
Audience: founder
Blocked by: —

## What needs doing

Get a corpus of the founder describing a real week's availability, in his own words. Voice memos are ideal — a transcript loses the hesitations and self-corrections, and the audio itself becomes the seed of a v0.2 STT eval set. Text is acceptable if voice is a hassle.

Aim for enough variety to be useful, not a research programme:

- Several weeks if possible, including a peak week and a lean one.
- Hard cases the parser will actually hit: hedged quantities ("maybe 30 lbs", "a good amount"), multiple products in one breath, negations ("no more squash"), future-dated claims ("done with tomatoes til next month"), and his own units ("a mess of", flats, bushels).
- Whatever he'd say to a regular customer who asked what he has — that phrasing is the target, not a form-filling voice.

## Why this is a task, not a decision

Nothing to decide. But [The normalisation target shape](06-normalisation-target-shape.md), [The check-in conversation](07-check-in-conversation.md), and [How we know the parser is right](18-parser-eval.md) are all guessing until this exists. It also overlaps with the founder questions on units and freshness — collect it in the same conversation.

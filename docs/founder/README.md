# Founder briefings

One file per topic we want the founder thinking about **before** it becomes work.

This is not the question board. Open questions about what we are building now
live in [`NORTHSTAR.md`](../../NORTHSTAR.md) as callouts, answered in place and
folded into the text once settled. A briefing here is the other direction: a
subject that is probably a real part of the product one day, where he knows
things we don't and we know things he hasn't seen.

Each one should give him enough to think with and then get out of the way:

- **What we already hold**, in plain terms — what the system has been quietly
  recording, and what could be got out of it without asking a farmer for
  anything new.
- **What we don't know**, stated as what it would change if we knew.
- **Prompts**, in his register, that he can answer in a sentence or in a
  conversation. Never a list of features to approve.

No implementation detail unless it changes what the product does, what it
promises a farmer, or what it costs to be wrong about.
